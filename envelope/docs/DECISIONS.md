| D-031 | 2026-10-27 | **Map cut faces and a second source for sensitive receptors.** (1) Clipping the 3D tiles over the plot cut neighbouring buildings at the plot line and showed their hollow, black insides above the 2.4 m hoarding. | D-033 | 2026-10-27 | **Clip the 3D tiles under the building only (supersedes part 1 of D-031).** Cutting the whole plot out of the photogrammetry left a sunken plot with walls even after the cut faces were closed, because the plot floor sat at one sampled height while the real ground around it varies. | D-036 | 2026-10-26 | **Cited report narrative (RAG).** A corpus of Indian regulations and method documents (`corpus/manifest.json`, PDFs in gitignored `corpus/raw/`) is chunked and embedded at build time with bge-small-en-v1.5 (local ONNX, no paid API) into a committed `corpus/index.json` (~0.5 MB). The server retrieves per section with precomputed query vectors + BM25 fused by RRF, sends passages as "sources" next to the payload, and the LLM cites them as [S1]. The validator drops unknown citations; regulatory claims without one are sent back in the retry and then dropped; the template writer cites fixed documents. Cited passages are listed under References. Numbers rule unchanged: passages never add allowed numbers. Precomputed query vectors keep the Vercel function free of an ML runtime; index loads in ~50 ms. |
| D-037 | 2026-10-26 | **Retrieval queries follow the report; passages are budgeted per provider.** Each section keeps its fixed queries (precomputed vectors, relevance floor) and adds BM25 keyword queries built from the payload (compliance rows, EIA built-up area, exceedances, background PM10, main dust sources, measures, receptors, limitations); a query naming a corpus document by alias searches that document. | D-038 | 2026-10-27 | **Station-corrected background PM (NCR).** LightGBM on CAMS + ERA5 features predicts CPCB station PM10/PM2.5 (OpenAQ archive, Feb 2025–Oct 2026, 58 stations; 6 Gurugram/Manesar stations held out).
